01 · About

Problem First. Product Second.

Most products fail by solving the stated problem instead of the real one. I start there. I frame the problem, define the users and the outcome, cut scope to an MVP and own delivery until the product works in the real world.

The domain changes. The discipline does not: clear requirements, explicit trade-offs, tested releases and iteration driven by real use.

Being AI-native means I can prototype, build and validate without waiting for a full engineering cycle. It also makes me a sharper partner to engineers, because I know what it takes to build what I specify.
