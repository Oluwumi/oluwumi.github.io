One rule: 70% attendance plus a capstone project to graduate. Attendance was tracked by hand, so nobody knew their standing until it was too late.

I translated the rule into an automated eligibility engine and a reporting layer that told every stakeholder where they stood, in English or French. The Fort Institute ran it for the Foundational Leadership Development Course (FLDC), Cohort 6.

Delivered

An automated eligibility engine with role-based reporting in English and French.

Adopted

Used for all 4,001 students, their facilitators, 80 fort leaders and programme leadership, through to the final graduation list.

Impact

Over 700 hours of manual work saved, by a conservative estimate, and an At Risk warning before students lost eligibility.

02The Problem

  • Manual attendance tracking across 4,001 students and 40 forts (learning groups).
  • No early warning before a student lost eligibility.
  • A slow, error-prone final graduation list.

03Users

Students · 4,001

Need to know their standing and what it takes to graduate.

Facilitators

Need attendance and honest feedback on their sessions.

Fort Leaders · 80

A governor and a deputy for each fort, who need their group's health at a glance.

Programme Leadership

Cohort president and vice president, dean, sub-dean and head of graduate success need a course-wide view.

04Eligibility Rules

Graduation needs 7 of 10 modules. I turned that into three statuses tied directly to the rule.

05How It Works

IdentityAdmission NumbersI introduced them: a cohort prefix plus a serial, emailed with the handbook.
CaptureModule FormsEach module's attendance form feeds its own tab.
Source Of TruthOne DashboardJoins student records with every module tab.
RulesEligibility EngineOn Track, At Risk or Out for every student.
DeliveryRouted ReportsBy role and by language, after every session.

Reporting For Every Stakeholder

  • Students get an update after every session: sessions attended and sessions still needed. At-risk alerts, perfect attendance recognition and end-of-programme summaries complete the set. Students marked Out drop out of routine updates.
  • Facilitators get an attendance summary, anonymised feedback and a thank-you for each fort and module.
  • Fort leaders get their attendance rate, module trend and status mix.
  • Leadership gets the course-wide view.
  • Every message is routed in English or French, and sending is spread across organisation accounts to stay within 1,500 emails per account per day.

07Result

700+Hours of manual work saved*
4,001Students tracked through to the final list

* A conservative estimate of manual work removed, not an audited measurement. It counts only work that certainly happened: 4,001 admission emails and 12,003 weekly student updates at 2 minutes each, 400 facilitator reports at 15 minutes each and 400 fort leader reports at 10 minutes each. Later updates, leadership reports and alerts are excluded.

08Lesson

Automate the repetitive work so people can focus on what matters. Over 700 hours of manual emails and reports were handed to the system.
