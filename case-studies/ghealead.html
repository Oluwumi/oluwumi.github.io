01Overview

A client asked for a trauma recovery course. I saw that content alone could not deliver recovery: people needed screening, a qualified therapist and continuity of care.

I reframed the product as a virtual therapy platform, wrote the requirements, owned the product decisions across every journey and tested it through to release. It is live at ghealead.com and has not yet been publicly launched.

02The Reframe

The AskA trauma recovery course.
The Real Job To Be DoneGet a person safely from "I need help" to ongoing sessions with the right therapist.

A course could not answer the questions that mattered. Who is suitable for virtual therapy? Who decides? How is a client matched, charged, booked and cared for over time?

Those are the questions of a service, not of content. I expanded the scope from content to service.

Trade-Off

A larger MVP and a longer build, in exchange for a product that could actually deliver recovery support.

03Users

Clients

Need a safe route into care, a clear status at every step and control over their sessions.

Therapists

Need their availability, their assigned clients and a place to document care.

Admins

Run intake, matching, payments, sessions and access. Permissions are set per role, and the lead therapist works as an admin-type user.

04The Core Journey

  1. ScreenThe client registers, verifies their email, completes a pre-assessment and accepts the terms. It is a suitability screen, not a diagnosis.
  2. ReviewThe lead therapist approves or declines each application in the admin pipeline.
    If Declined: the applicant automatically receives an email with partner care options.
  3. Approve And PayApproved clients choose a plan and pay through a payment gateway, in multiple currencies.
  4. MatchThe lead therapist assigns a therapist manually, guided by specialisation tags.
  5. BookThe client books from the therapist's availability. Current rule: one session per week. Rescheduling is supported and the therapist is notified by email.
  6. DeliverAn admin attaches a Google Meet link and both parties join from the app.
  7. DocumentThe therapist logs notes, progress and attendance or no-shows.
  8. TrackThe client dashboard shows sessions purchased, completed and remaining, plus progress.

All notifications go by email.

06PRD Excerpt

Product Requirements

Reconstructed For This Portfolio

Objective: enable clients to apply for virtual trauma recovery support, pass suitability screening, pay for an approved plan and receive ongoing sessions with a matched therapist.

US1 · Apply

As a prospective client, I want to complete a pre-assessment so that my application can be reviewed.

Acceptance: email verified · required fields complete · terms accepted before submission · application enters the pending queue · status visible to the client.

US2 · Review

As the lead therapist, I want to approve or decline applications so that only suitable clients proceed.

Acceptance: pending applications listed · approval triggers the payment email · decline triggers the referral email.

US3 · Match

As the lead therapist, I want to assign a therapist to a paid client so that they can book.

Acceptance: therapists listed with specialisations · assignment is manual · therapist linked to the client record.

US4 · Book

As a client, I want to book from my therapist's availability so that I can start sessions.

Acceptance: open slots shown · booking and rescheduling supported · therapist emailed on booking · admin can attach a meeting link.

US5 · Document

As a therapist, I want to record notes and attendance so that care is continuous.

Acceptance: notes saved per session · attendance or no-show marked · history retained.
