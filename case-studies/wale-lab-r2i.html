A 10-week research fellowship with a weighted credit system and a hard attendance bar. Attendance sat in about 20 separate form tabs, and fellows typed in their own details.

I owned the product end to end, from requirements to data architecture to build, iterating while the cohort was live for Wale Lab, the research and innovation hub of Wale University. The core requirement: no data error should decide who graduates.

Dummy DataEvery visual on this page is recreated from the live product with invented fellows, IDs, scores and figures.

02The Problem

  • Attendance fragmented across about 20 form tabs.
  • Fellows could fall below the bar before anyone noticed.
  • Messy self-entered data: reversed names, middle names, student ID typos and mismatched emails.
  • Feedback that had to reach only the right track, STEM or SHAPE.
  • No weekly view of programme health.

03Design Principles

Early Warnings Change Behaviour

Tell fellows the same day, while they can still recover.

Never Trust Self-Entered Data As-Is

Matching must tolerate human error.

Feedback Must Reach The Right People

Route it by track, or it becomes noise.

Admins Need A Digest

Not another spreadsheet to remember to check.

No Code To Operate

Every routine task runs from a menu.

04How It Works

Module 1Attendance EngineReads every session's responses and marks Present or Absent.
Module 2Four-Pass MatchingResolves reversed names, middle names, ID typos and mismatched emails.
Module 3Grading DashboardScore entry feeds automatic grades and tiers.
Module 4Feedback RoutingSeven-point ratings routed to the right track.
Module 5Admin DigestNo-code menu and a Monday summary.

Every evening, each fellow receives Attendance Confirmed with a running count, or Absence Recorded with a 48-hour correction window. Standing: On Track (0 to 2 missed), At Risk (3 to 4) and Out (5 or more). Fellows marked Out leave routine updates. Anything still unmatched goes to admins as a Flagged Submission Alert before the window closes.

06Key Decisions And Trade-Offs

DecisionWhyTrade-Off
Four-pass matchingExact match marks real attendees absentMore complexity, with edge cases left for review
Raw responses as the source of truthA live backfill bug showed the log could driftMore reconciliation logic
Split live and backfill processingLive never overwrites; backfill can correctTwo modes to maintain
Convert word ratings to a 1 to 4 scaleSessions become comparableA fixed mapping
Hosted email iconsGmail strips embedded SVGsA dependency on an external asset
No merged cellsFrozen columns kept breakingLess layout freedom

07Result

70+Hours of manual work saved*
164Fellows tracked, graded and tiered from one source of truth

* A conservative estimate of manual work removed, not an audited measurement. It counts only the attendance emails every fellow was certain to receive at 2 minutes each, plus session feedback reports at 15 minutes each and weekly summaries at 30 minutes each. Matching and flagged-submission handling are excluded.

08What I Would Do Next

  • A self-service fellow dashboard for attendance, scores and standing.
  • Automatic mentor escalation when a fellow reaches At Risk.
  • Automated verification of social media posts.
  • A reusable template for future cohorts.
